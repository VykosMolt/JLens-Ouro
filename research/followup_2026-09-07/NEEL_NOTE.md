After the application I checked every layer, compared the exit lenses with Ouro's actual outputs, and split items by whether the answer passed. The main negative result survived: J-Lens loses on multihop questions in the first three loops, including in the middle of the stack and on successful items. There is a small favorable region in the final pass, but it needs replication. The striking disagreement between exit lenses was mostly a poor measure of model revision: loop-3 and final predictions agree about 85%, while the lenses agree about 1%. The supervised probe still loses in loop 1 after cheap checks; too little data is plausible, not established, and it wins in loop 3. I also found two remaining answer-matcher false positives.
