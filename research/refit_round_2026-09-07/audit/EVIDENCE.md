These shards could support a clearly labeled exploratory N24 partition sensitivity analysis. They cannot establish repeated-fit variation at N100: they are deterministic contiguous partitions, adjacent partitions share articles, and each partition covers only 3–5 articles. Additional retained N8 and N20 shards complete the historical N100 fit. Reweighting these shards or subtracting nested fitted means cannot create fresh independent calibration samples, and fp16 serialization also prevents treating those operations as exact recovery of original per-prompt matrices.

No retained per-prompt Jacobian matrices or running-sum checkpoint binaries were found in the inspected locations. The logs contain per-prompt scalar diagnostics, not the matrices. The [fit implementation](/home/moloch/jacobian-lens/jlens/fitting.py:280) accumulates per-prompt matrices into a float32 sum and overwrites a checkpoint with the cumulative sum, count, and cursor. The [saved lens format](/home/moloch/jacobian-lens/jlens/lens.py:52) stores the final mean in fp16 by default. Thus an evaluation-prompt bootstrap quantifies evaluation sampling uncertainty conditional on a fixed fitted lens; it does not quantify calibration-fit uncertainty. A bootstrap over the few retained shard means would likewise resample those particular partitions rather than provide new independent N100 fits.

The current wrapper [fit_lens.py](/home/moloch/ouro_project/src/ouro_jlens/fit_lens.py:1273) fits a selected contiguous prompt slice, validates the exact fitted count, and merges only compatible, nonoverlapping contiguous shards with count weighting. Its target selection exposes loop-end states. At the estimator level, [jacobian_for_prompt](/home/moloch/jacobian-lens/jlens/fitting.py:175) places the cotangent at every valid target position, sums their gradients, and takes a mean over valid source positions. Valid positions are indices `16:seq_len-1`; the final prompt token is excluded. Changing the target to a penultimate layer or changing the position reduction needs new Jacobians. A final-target position-averaged matrix does not retain the information needed to reconstruct either variant.
