# Claims after the follow-up

7 September 2026. Verdicts refer to the stated scope. “Supported” does not imply causality or generalization beyond these tasks and fitted readouts. Numerical intervals are 95% and pointwise unless described otherwise.

| Claim | Evidence | Strongest counterargument or limit | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final-exit J-Lens loses to the logit lens on multihop content in Ouro loops 1–3. | Independent reproduction of any-layer differences −.356, −.479, −.407; all three intervals below zero. Middle/late layer bands and control, item-exclusion and cluster sensitivities preserve the loss. [Results](results/layerwise.json), [sensitivities](audit/sensitivities.md). | One model, curated labels, one fitted estimator; not a claim about every concept or fit. | **supported** |
| The main multihop loss is merely a consequence of best-of-48 aggregation letting the logit lens select late layers. | At fixed middle layers 17–32 the first-three-loop differences are −.071, −.114, −.154, with negative item and concept-cluster intervals. Loop 2 has a negative mean at all 48 layers. | Aggregation still hides the final-pass positive region and some arithmetic advantages. | **rejected** |
