## Question and estimand

Does the original main Ouro fit01 retain its localized advantage over raw logit lens on new multihop questions and intermediate concept identities? The discovery population consists of the earlier 93 multihop questions (90 eligible); its results are kept separate. The primary estimator is the originally preselected fit01, not the best of five fits. Its intact FP32 checkpoint-to-FP16 bank conversion passed a complete independent numerical comparison.

For eligible item i, eligible label s, and layer v, let I(m,i,s,v) be one if any accepted single-token form of the intended label has zero-based full-vocabulary rank below 10 under method m. Let C(m,i,s,v) be the equally weighted mean of that indicator over the fixed matched control names. The item score is the mean of I−C over its eligible labels. The paired primary item value is the mean of [score(fit01)−score(raw)] over the twelve fixed virtual layers 169 through 180. The primary estimate is the equally weighted mean over eligible items. Layers, labels, token aliases and calibration fits are not independent observations.

## Population, precision and stopping

The fixed target is 160 eligible questions, comprising 80 new intermediate-concept identities and two substantively distinct questions per identity. A concept family is the pair sharing an intermediate identity and its true semantic aliases. The dependency grouping additionally connects shared underlying propositions, concrete near-duplicate or entity-substitution constructions, and normalized final-answer collisions. Broad domain, common publisher, generic clue-to-entity-to-property format, and isolated broad relation types do not alone define a dependency edge. All connected components of the explicit audited graph are resampled as units.

The blinded dependency audit resolves the questions into 28 connected components, with sizes 42, 24, 16, 10, 10, 6, four groups of 4 and eighteen groups of 2. The weighted effective group count N²/Σn_g² is 8.6253. For assumed SDs of paired group means 0.30, 0.45 and 0.60, the planning 95% half-widths are approximately 0.200, 0.300 and 0.400. The initial approximately 0.10 precision target at SD 0.45 is not met. These are dependence/variance scenarios, not an assumed effect, a realized confidence interval, or a power guarantee. We retain the fixed 160-question design before any new outcomes because it still estimates transfer to a new, explicitly defined population; we accept that it may leave the sign and magnitude unresolved. We will not describe this design as achieving the initial precision target, alter dependency edges, change item weights, or add questions after seeing results. Eligibility exclusions require recomputing the graph and planning quantities before final freeze.

The curated relation mixture consists of 45 questions with a familiar requested-relation family, 19 recombining familiar fact relations in a different hop or requested slot, and 96 whose typed requested relation was not represented in the 93 discovery prompts. It therefore tests new-concept/new-question transfer across this fixed mixture, including a substantial relation shift. The common factual clue-to-entity-to-property task form provides structural comparability, while equivalent empirical difficulty is not established and will not be enforced by filtering on model outcomes. Neither the familiar-only subset nor a favorable relation subgroup may replace the primary endpoint. Group bootstrap and influence summaries are conditional on this curated population and fit, with limited information from large unequal groups; finite-sample coverage across all possible multihop tasks or relation mixtures is not established.

Independent annotation review checks both factual hops against sources and ensures the intermediate is not supplied at readout. All discovery intermediate identities, including previously ineligible labels and true semantic aliases, are excluded. Discovery prompt/fact overlap and known calibration-text overlap are audited and recorded. Three known component-fact overlaps are explicitly retained: item076's nuclear double membrane (fit03 paragraph8), item130's kelvin/temperature relation (fit01 paragraph42), and item151's monsoon timing (fit01 paragraph12). These are new two-hop questions and new discovery-intermediate identities, not a claim that every constituent fact was absent from calibration. The complete lexical audit also retains incidental mentions for inspection. There is no claim of absence from model pretraining and no claim that an annotated intermediate was actually used by the model.

Only factual, novelty, boundary, alias/control-hygiene and predeclared eligibility checks may exclude or replace candidates before freeze. No selection uses Ouro's answer, either readout's recovery, or a method contrast. The final fixed population is evaluated once. There is no sequential sample expansion after intervals are inspected.
